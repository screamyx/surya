## Three reasons a senior designer sends this back

1. The dark theme does not lift its panels. In `shell-dark-1440x900.png` the rail, the feed and the canvas are one brown; the floating-panel idea of decision 22 exists in light only.
2. The accent is everywhere and therefore nowhere: list numbers, inline code, file paths, code blocks, tab underlines. When "Approve" arrives it is a cream button, not the accent.
3. No page-title tier and no rail entries: the brief's editorial header and decision 11's Home / Needs you / Agents / Tasks are absent, so the shell is still comet with a warmer palette.

## What is actually good

- The composer pill: floating, one dark disc for stop, quiet harness label. This is the sketch.
- Light mode's material: parchment canvas, ivory panels, ring-like hairlines, no gradient. The brief's Anthropic anchor is recognisable at a glance.

## What I could not judge

- Interaction truth: nothing was clicked. Drag, tabs, menus, the edit sheet are unverified in this round.
- Phone width: the window will not go below 900x600.
- The files card on the right pane and the browser pane: not on main.
- Motion and reduced motion: still frames only.
- The mock repeat joins segments without a blank line ("Mock harness reporting in.## Streaming pipeline"); that is the rig, not the renderer.
